## 1. Grimmelt--Merikoski Type I/II input

Source: L. Grimmelt and J. Merikoski, *On the greatest prime factor and
uniform equidistribution of quadratic polynomials*, arXiv:2505.00493v2,
30 May 2025.

Locked file and SHA-256:

```text
b88e92a2f1849433799f4ce935871f9488f03b660b96e60b5598a4aabc50e53d
tmp/pdfs/grimmelt-merikoski-2505.00493.pdf
```

- PDF p. 22, Corollary 7.1: for `D <= X^(1/2-eta)`, it bounds the sum over
  `d <= D` of the absolute modulus-`d` discrepancy.  This is the precise
  input needed to collect variable Rosser weights and boundedly many prime
  prefix representations.
- PDF pp. 22--23, Corollary 7.2: if `M >= N`, `MN=X^alpha`, and

  ```text
  X^(alpha-1+2*eta) <= N <= X^((2-alpha)/3-4*eta/3),
  ```

  the Type-II discrepancy has a fixed power saving for divisor-bounded
  coefficients, with the `N` coefficient supported on squarefree integers.
- PDF p. 2, Theorem 1.1 and the paragraph immediately following it: the
  auxiliary real-character hypothesis is unconditional when `a*h <= X^eps`.
  The manuscript uses only `a=h=1`.
- PDF p. 23: the authors explicitly state that Corollaries 7.1 and 7.2
  recover the Type-I/II ranges previously available under Selberg's
  eigenvalue conjecture and invoke Merikoski's proof of Theorem 2.

Status: exact statement match.  No endpoint is used; the manuscript inserts
fixed strict margins.

## 2. Merikoski sieve framework

Source: J. Merikoski, *On the largest prime factor of `n^2+1`*, J. Eur.
Math. Soc. 25 (2023), 1253--1284, DOI `10.4171/JEMS/1216`.

Locked author PDF and SHA-256:

```text
29f5c7c7e42e480a931e5ca9545b7ec837832c1d64c1bee28041a5cb4878c118
tmp/pdfs/merikoski-1908.08816.pdf
```

- PDF p. 5, Proposition 3: Type-I information for arbitrary
  divisor-bounded coefficients; the preceding paragraph explains why the
  power saving upgrades bounded coefficients to divisor-bounded ones.
- PDF p. 6, Proposition 4(i): Type-II asymptotic for divisor-bounded
  coefficients whose `N` coefficient is squarefree-supported.
- PDF p. 8, Proposition 6: Fundamental Proposition II for divisor-bounded
  prefix coefficients, with its exact `U` and `gamma` parameters.
- PDF p. 8: the proof of Proposition 6 explicitly uses Perron inversion to
  remove the cross-condition between consecutive prime factors.
- PDF p. 9, equation (2.5): `omega(u) <= 0.5617` for `u>=4` and
  `omega(u) >= 0.5607` for `u>=2.47`.
- PDF p. 9, Lemma 7: transformation of well-behaved ordered prime regions
  into the Buchstab integrals and normalization used in the manuscript.
- PDF pp. 13--14, Section 2.4.3: three Buchstab expansions and the explicit
  statement that any combination of `q1,q2,q3` in the Type-II range may be
  selected.
- PDF p. 14, Remark 12: the additional Type-II sums obtained by further
  Buchstab expansion are explicitly identified as available but unused in
  the published calculation.
- PDF pp. 20--21, Section 3.4: Mellin removal of the smooth product
  cross-conditions before application of the Type-II estimate.

Status: exact structural match.  The new manuscript uses the same finite
cross-condition mechanism with an explicit half-integer Perron threshold.
